MayorClips

AI clipping and multi-network publishing, running on your own machine.

MayorClips is a self-hosted desktop tool for creators. It finds highlight moments in long streams, renders vertical 9:16 clips with subtitles and camera layouts, and publishes them to the creator's own social accounts through official platform APIs.

What the app does

  • Analyses long videos and live streams to detect highlight moments.
  • Renders short vertical clips locally with FFmpeg.
  • Connects the creator's own accounts with official OAuth flows (Login Kit).
  • Publishes clips directly to the creator's TikTok profile (Direct Post) or uploads drafts to their creator inbox for review.

Where your data lives

MayorClips is not a hosted service. Videos, tokens and settings stay on the machine where you run it. When you connect TikTok, the app talks directly to TikTok's official API using the credentials you provide. No account data is sent to us.
